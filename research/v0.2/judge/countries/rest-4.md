You are one of several independent judges for a public forecasting dashboard, Doom Analysis. Give your own calibrated probabilities. Do not anchor on any number you believe the site's author wants. Do not use tools; reason from the evidence below and your own knowledge.

## Definitions (fixed)
- Horizon: every event is judged by the end of 2100.
- The five essentials are energy (reliable electricity and clean cooking), fresh water (safe drinking water and sanitation), food (enough nutritious food), health (care that keeps life expectancy at or above 75 years), and security (physical safety from war, violence and persecution).
- Abundance: A place reaches abundance in 2100 if three things hold. First, at least 90% of its residents have reliable access to all five essentials. Second, the essentials are nearly free: together they cost less than 10% of national income, counting public and private spending, averaged over 2096–2100 so that a shock year counts. Third, it never collapsed along the way.
- Why abundance is defined this strictly: This is the strong version of the abundance claim: that superintelligence and robots make energy, food, water, health care and security so cheap that nobody has to worry about them, "universal high income" rather than a safety net. Meeting basic needs is not enough; rich countries already do that for most people. Near-free is the test.
- Collapse: A place collapses if, at any point before 2100, a majority of its residents lose reliable access to at least one of the five essentials for five years or more. Losing a single essential is enough. Global doom counts as collapse everywhere.
- Doom: Global doom means human extinction, or an irreversible civilization-wide collapse before 2100: most of humanity loses the five essentials, and global civilization is not expected to regain its 2025 capabilities within a century.
- Muddling through is everything else: no collapse, but not abundance either. Most places today would be muddling through by this standard.
- Superintelligence (SI) means AI systems that outperform the best humans at virtually every cognitive task, including science, engineering and strategy, and that are deployed widely enough to act in the economy. “If superintelligence arrives” assumes this happens before 2100; “if it never does” assumes it does not.

## Consistency rules (your answer is rejected if it breaks them)
- Every value is a probability between 0 and 1.
- abundance + collapse ≤ 1 for every place and scenario; abundance + doom ≤ 1 for the world.
- collapse ≥ the largest pillarLoss and ≤ the sum of the pillarLoss values.
- A place's collapse is at least the world's doom in the same scenario.

## For each place, for each scenario ("si": superintelligence arrives before 2100; "noSi": it does not)
- collapse: probability the place collapses before 2100 (including through global doom).
- abundance: probability the place reaches abundance, as strictly defined above, in 2100.
- pillarLoss: for each essential, the probability that losing it alone would trigger collapse before 2100.
- rationale: one or two sentences naming what matters most. Plain words.
