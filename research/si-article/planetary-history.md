# Planetary limits, ocean acidification, historical collapse

Research cutoff/check: 2026-11-01. No repository edits. Suggested article text below is a draft; citations and numeric provenance follow. Modern and ancient case studies support mechanisms, not an estimated frequency of global doom.

## Suggested compact main-text insertion (about 300 words)

### A planet has more than one limit

Climate is one of nine processes in the planetary-boundaries framework: the others concern ecosystems, land, fresh water, nitrogen and phosphorus, ocean chemistry, ozone, aerosols and novel pollutants. The September 2026 assessment classifies seven as outside its safe operating space. These are precautionary guardrails around interacting systems, not seven clocks counting down to extinction. Crossing a boundary means increasing risk; it is not the same thing as triggering a tipping point. [PIK 2026](https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/news/latest-news/planetary-health-check-2026-finds-mounting-pressures-across-earth2019s-life-support-systems), [framework explanation](https://www.planetaryhealthcheck.org/planetary-boundaries/).

Ocean acidification makes the distinction concrete. CO₂ dissolving in seawater increases hydrogen-ion activity and reduces carbonate available to corals and shellfish. A pH fall of 0.10 sounds small; because pH is logarithmic it means **10^0.10 ≈ 1.26 times the hydrogen-ion activity**. The water remains alkaline. The danger begins long before it could become an acid in the everyday sense. The planetary indicator is aragonite saturation, a measure of the chemical conditions for building calcium-carbonate structures. Regional exposure and species responses matter more than a single global average. [NOAA pH](https://pmel.noaa.gov/co2/story/A+primer+on+pH), [ocean boundary](https://www.planetaryhealthcheck.org/boundary/ocean-acidification/).

SI could improve monitoring, agriculture and restoration. It cannot replace lost species or make chemical and ecological recovery instantaneous. Solar geoengineering, for example, would not remove the CO₂ driving this chemistry. Some pressures can be reversed; the recovery clock can be much slower than the innovation clock. [IPCC carbon-cycle assessment](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-5/).

History suggests studying that gap without inventing a law of collapse. At Mayapan, drought coincided with factional violence and the loss of a political centre, while institutions and commerce continued elsewhere in the region. Roman climate-collapse explanations remain actively contested. The lesson to test is that stresses propagate through food, trade and political legitimacy. Neither an abandoned city nor a fallen empire is a human-extinction observation. [Mayapan](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-31522-x), [Roman methodological dispute](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10584-026-04112-9).

### 2. What a planetary boundary means

**Planetary Health Check framework explainer**, live page checked 2026-11-01:
https://www.planetaryhealthcheck.org/planetary-boundaries/

Control variables summarize Earth-system functions; passing a precautionary boundary enters increasing risk, while a tipping point is a qualitative system transition. Within-boundary global conditions do not eliminate local harm. Neither 7/9 nor normalized “distance beyond boundary” converts directly into P(doom). Aggregating processes into a count loses severity, uncertainty, spatial pattern and interactions. The framework helps specify what to monitor; it does not provide a forecast of societal collapse.

### 3. Ocean chemistry, didactically precise

**NOAA PMEL, A primer on pH**, checked 2026-11-01:
https://pmel.noaa.gov/co2/story/A+primer+on+pH

Historical global surface-ocean pH decline approximately 0.11 since industrialization, roughly 30% higher hydrogen-ion concentration/activity. Our arithmetic: pH = -log10(a_H+); a decrease of 0.10 multiplies activity by 10^0.10 = 1.2589, i.e. +25.9%; 0.11 gives 1.288, +28.8%. Specify 0.10→26% or 0.11→29% (rounded 30%); do not write 0.10→exactly30%. Concentration shorthand neglects activity-coefficient/pH-scale details, adequate for this illustration. Surface seawater remains around pH8, alkaline. Natural local variability can exceed the global mean trend; that does not remove a chronic shift in the baseline.

Simple carbonate explanation (our chemistry derivation):
CO₂ + H₂O ⇌ H⁺ + HCO₃⁻;
H⁺ + CO₃²⁻ ⇌ HCO₃⁻.
Net: CO₂ + H₂O + CO₃²⁻ ⇌ 2HCO₃⁻.

Thus adding dissolved CO₂ shifts carbon toward bicarbonate and away from carbonate. Aragonite saturation can be written Ω_arag = [Ca²⁺][CO₃²⁻]/Ksp(T,S,P), using a consistent seawater concentration convention. It is dimensionless. Ω=1 is chemical saturation; below1 exposed aragonite dissolution becomes thermodynamically favourable. Biology, protective tissues and local chemical regulation complicate organism response. Ω>1 is not a guarantee of ecological safety; calcification can become harder before undersaturation. Distinguish this chemical threshold from the global precautionary boundary.

### 4. Current acidification boundary metric and baseline

**Planetary Health Check ocean-specific page**, updated assessment checked 2026-11-01:
https://www.planetaryhealthcheck.org/boundary/ocean-acidification/

Sets global mean surface Ω boundary at **2.86**, 80% of a precautionary preindustrial **3.57**. Its map compares 2024 observation-based reconstruction with1750; largest relative declines at high latitudes, while regional conditions differ. Reports formal classification as seventh breached boundary in2025. The year of classification is not the first year any location suffered damage. Ω=2.86 global boundary, Ω=1 chemical saturation, and pH7 are three different ideas. The “80%” is relative Ω, not 80% of pH, not 20% loss of ocean biodiversity.

### 5. Peer-reviewed 2025 acidification reassessment: crucial uncertainty

**Findlay et al., Global Change Biology, 9 June 2025**, DOI10.1111/gcb.70238:
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/gcb.70238
Full text inspected (Wiley; PMC later hit CAPTCHA).

For2020, reconstructed global surface Ω=**2.90±0.06**; relative decline **17.3±5.0%**, overlapping original **20±5.3%** boundary uncertainty. Central global estimate alone did not clearly exceed original20%. Using precautionary high baseline, ~40% surface area and ~60% at200m exceed20% regional decline. Habitat-suitability calculations are not direct counts of organisms killed or reefs destroyed. Authors recommend a tighter10% boundary. These methodological changes and later observations help explain formal classification; retain provenance if quoting numbers. Avoid “global ocean suddenly crossed20% in2025” or “60% of all ocean volume is dead.”

**NOAA research account, 11 June2025**:
https://oceanacidification.noaa.gov/study-finds-ocean-acidification-is-more-pervasive-than-previously-thought/

Provides observed consequences and examples of vulnerable calcifiers; emphasizes multiple pressures and correlation limits in wild population studies. Pacific Northwest shellfish hatcheries illustrate practical adaptation through monitoring and buffering intake water. A protected hatchery is a small controlled volume; scaling to an open ocean is a different intervention. Biological impacts vary; the mechanism is established more firmly than precise future ecosystem-wide loss estimates.

### 6. What SI could and could not reverse

**IPCC AR6 WGI, Chapter5 (2021), especially5.3.4.2 and FAQ5.3**:
https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-5/

CO₂ removal can reverse surface-ocean acidification; recovery at depth can take centuries. Different properties respond on different clocks, and removing atmospheric CO₂ partly induces compensating ocean/land fluxes. Solar radiation modification does not remove the carbon driving acidification. High emissions stopped later are not physically equivalent to emissions avoided earlier. Do not say all ocean acidification is irreversible, nor that reaching net-zero instantly restores preindustrial chemistry. Species extinctions, unlike pH, have no simple reverse operation. Main science accessible through indexed official text; direct IPCC access may403 as in prior research.

SI relevance (our inference): better catalysts, low-carbon energy, carbon-removal process design and sensing can help. They still need energy, feedstocks, installation and verification. Ocean alkalinity enhancement is a research/intervention candidate, not a free proven planetary cure; additional environmental assessment and field evidence would be needed. No detailed discussion necessary in short article.

## Historical cases: mechanisms, uncertainty, survival

### 7. Classic Maya drought: a measured stress, not a sufficient cause

**Evans et al., Science, 3August2018**, DOI10.1126/science.aas9871:
https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aas9871
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30072537/
Author repository: https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/22e363fe-679b-4410-9a99-3996a2e516a8

Gypsum-water isotopes from Lake Chichancanab + hydrological model reconstruct annual precipitation **41–54% below present-day**, peak reductions **up to70%**, during Terminal Classic (~800–1000CE). This is one regional proxy/model, not a uniform percentage across all Maya lands; proxy and chronology uncertainty matter. It establishes severe drought coincident with political/demographic disruption, not the fraction of collapse caused by drought. Classic Maya city-state political collapse is not disappearance of Maya peoples or the collapse of all society everywhere.

### 8. Mayapan: interacting causes and regional persistence

**Kennett et al., Nature Communications, 19July2022**, DOI10.1038/s41467-022-31522-x:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-31522-x
Full text retrieved (canonical URL first failed; accessible rendered text confirmed).

Archaeological/historical/paleoclimate study of Mayapan, especially drought and civil strife1400–1450. Authors link drought with increased factional conflict, trauma and eventual abandonment; regional political/economic structures endured until European contact. They directly dated205 human skeletons, integrated climate proxies and documentary evidence. Correlations and interpreted mechanisms do not prove climate alone determined outcomes. This later Postclassic case is **not** the Terminal Classic collapse800–1000; do not merge the dates. Main-text value: the failure of one political centre can coexist with regional adaptation and commerce. This is the cleanest compact example for distinguishing state collapse from extinction.

### 10. Counterweight: resilience is also in the archaeological record

**Riris et al., Nature, online1May2024**, DOI10.1038/s41586-024-07354-8:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07354-8
Author repository https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/9cfd0fac-e4c3-4953-9da4-40826aff54e1

Meta-analysis of16 regional radiocarbon-based population reconstructions spanning~30,000years finds frequent prior disturbances associated with greater resistance/recovery. Proxy/case-selection limitations prevent a universal causal law, and result is not an argument to create shocks. Useful rebuttal to selecting only collapsed empires: populations often adapted, moved and reorganized. Archaeological decline is not automatically complete technological or biological erasure.

## Suggested probability update treatment

Keep planetary status in the dated evidence ledger. A new boundary classification may reflect new observations, revised baseline, or changed precautionary definition; record which. Those imply different changes to belief. Under no circumstances map7/9 to78% doom, add these correlated processes as independent hazards, or infer P(global irreversible collapse) from number of collapsed historical states.

A meaningful update to doom/abundance should connect observable mechanism to exposure and response: degraded fisheries + nutritional dependence + weak replacement food capacity; synchronized drought + trade interruptions + institutional instability; failing carbon sinks + slower mitigation. Also record successful adaptation and restoration. Modern medicine, crop breeding, trade and forecasting improve resilience; global synchronization and tightly coupled supply chains can widen cascades. These are countervailing mechanisms to model, not proof that modernity guarantees either safety or collapse.

## Positive repair evidence: ozone

**UNEP Ozone Secretariat, Facts and figures on ozone protection**, checked2026-11-01:
https://ozone.unep.org/facts-and-figures-ozone-protection

Reports Montreal Protocol implementation phased out99% of controlled ozone-depleting substances. Upper-stratospheric ozone increased about1.5–2.2% per decade at midlatitudes during2000–2020; recovery varies by altitude/region. With continued compliance, assessed return of total ozone to1980 levels is around2040 for60°N–60°S,2045 Arctic,2066 Antarctic. Do not say ozone fully restored today or use the phaseout percentage for all ozone destruction. The measured policy response and ongoing recovery show that international institutions and substitute technologies can reduce a global pressure; future recovery remains conditional.

Suggested short balancing sentence: “Ozone is evidence that repair is possible: coordinated restrictions phased out99% of controlled ozone-depleting substances, and recovery is underway. It also shows the delay between successful intervention and full physical recovery.”
