# Doom, climate, resources: research memo

Evidence cutoff: 2026-11-01. Research checked 2026-11-01. Primary sources and official scientific syntheses only. Numbers below are observed estimates, modeled scenarios, or explicitly our calculations; none estimates P(doom|SI) directly.

## Recommended thesis and probability definitions

Intelligence can reduce the cost of searching for a solution; survival depends on whether the solution is physically deployable and whether the same intelligence amplifies destructive capabilities faster than defenses. Stronger AI is evidence about both branches.

Define D as human extinction or an irreversible loss of humanity's long-run capacity to recover a flourishing civilization. Keep a separate catastrophe outcome (e.g. >=10% global mortality or sustained global collapse) if that is also of interest. A disastrous heat wave, pandemic, or mineral shortage is not automatically D. Climate science does not provide a defensible direct numerical extinction probability.

Conditioning needs dates. S_2050 = SI exists by 2050; D_2100 = doom occurs by 2100. P(D_2100|S_2050) is interpretable; using 2050 for both is also valid but mixes very different exposure times for SI arriving in 2030 versus 2049. Show the choice. “Abundance by 2100” and “doom by 2100” can be mutually exclusive if abundance is an endpoint; “ever had abundance” can precede doom, so they then overlap. Do not enforce complementarity without defining it.

P(D|S) is all-cause risk in worlds containing SI. It is not the causal risk created by SI. A baseline P(D|no S) helps, but simple conditioning is confounded (peaceful, wealthy worlds may be more likely to develop SI). A causal counterfactual requires assumptions. Do not call P(D|S)-P(D|not S) “the AI-caused risk” without them.

For overlapping events war, disease, climate, resource failure, loss of control, max_i P(D_i|S) <= P(union_i D_i|S) <= sum_i P(D_i|S). Do not add percentages or multiply survival probabilities as if independent. Example cascade: war -> damaged grids -> crop failure -> disease. The same disaster should not be counted four times. Annual hazard h gives 1-exp(-hT) only under an explicit constant-hazard model; it is a sensitivity illustration, not observed frequency of unprecedented SI.

## Twelve core source records

1. **Jancovici, What is energy, actually?** Published 2011-08-01, modified 2015-08-01. https://jancovici.com/en/energy-transition/energy-and-us/what-is-energy-actually/ . Best use: the physical framing that production transforms matter using energy; software and information infrastructure remain material systems. It distinguishes heat, chemical transformations, mechanical motion, phase changes. Do not recycle its dated energy/GDP correlations as a universal law or treat GDP-energy proportionality as thermodynamics. No quotation needed.

2. **The Shift Project, Intelligence artificielle, données, calculs: quelles infrastructures dans un monde décarboné?** Final report 2025-10-01. https://theshiftproject.org/publications/intelligence-artificielle-centres-de-donnees-rapport-final/ . Scenario: data-center electricity could triple 2023–2030; life-cycle footprint could reach 920 MtCO2e/year in 2030, with manufacture/construction 25% of footprint. This is a scenario, not measured 2030 emissions. Its claimed ~1000TWh ceiling depends on its sector decarbonization assumptions, not a fundamental thermodynamic cap. Preserve boundaries: all data centers, AI subset; life-cycle CO2e differs from electricity-only CO2. Good Jancovici-adjacent source, but authored by Shift's digital team rather than Jancovici himself.

3. **IPCC AR6 WGI SPM**, 2021. https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/summary-for-policymakers/ . D.1: near-linear cumulative CO2 vs warming. Every 1000 GtCO2 causes best-estimate 0.45°C warming, likely range 0.27–0.63°C. D.1.8: global net-zero anthropogenic CO2 is required to stabilize CO2-induced temperature increase. Additional non-CO2 forcing matters. Search index verified exact passages; direct page returned 403 to browser. Citation is still canonical. Equation: delta T_CO2 ~= (0.45°C/1000 GtCO2) * cumulative net emissions. Valid as an order-of-magnitude approximation in assessed range, not a complete climate model.

4. **IPCC AR6 Synthesis SPM**, 2023. https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/syr/summary-for-policymakers/ . B.3: sea-level rise continues centuries to millennia owing to deep-ocean warming and ice loss, remaining elevated thousands of years. Distinguish temperature stabilization from sea-level stabilization. Climate risks rise with warming, but this report is not a numerical human-extinction forecast. Index passages verified; direct access returned 403. Avoid sensational conversion of 1.5°C overshoot into certain doom.

5. **Friedlingstein et al., Global Carbon Budget 2025**, final publication **2026-05-13**, ESSD 18, 3211. https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/18/3211/2026/ . For 2024: total anthropogenic CO2 42.4 ±3.2 GtCO2/year; fossil including cement carbonation 10.3 ±0.5GtC/year. Preliminary 2025 total 42.2GtCO2, fossil 38.1GtCO2. Paper labeled 2025 despite 2026 final publication. Round to 4e10 tonnes CO2/year for physical reasoning; distinguish CO2 from all greenhouse-gas CO2-equivalent emissions. At 40GtCO2/year another decade adds 400GtCO2 and roughly 0.18°C CO2-induced warming (likely TCRE range 0.11–0.25°C), a calculation not a full temperature forecast.

6. **IEA, Energy and AI: AI and climate change**, 2025-04-10 report. https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/ai-and-climate-change . Electricity-related indirect data-center CO2: ~180Mt in 2024, ~0.5% of combustion emissions; AI is a subset. Existing AI applications in end-use sectors could avoid 1400MtCO2/year in 2035 in Widespread Adoption Case. Adoption is not guaranteed; rebound can negate benefits. Its 1.4Gt potential saving is order 10^9t/year versus current order 4e10t/year total CO2 (~3% comparison, different years); helpful yet far short of solving the whole climate problem. Examples: transport efficiency 5–10%, building controls ~10%. Avoid comparing gross modeled savings with observed net impact.

7. **Keith et al., A Process for Capturing CO2 from the Atmosphere**, Joule 2018. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joule.2018.05.006 ; author-hosted copy https://davidkeith.earth/publication/a-process-for-capturing-co2-from-the-atmosphere/ . A specific liquid DAC process design (simulation supported by pilot data), with CO2 delivered at 15MPa: 5.25GJ natural gas +366kWh electricity per tonne captured, or 8.81GJ gas alternative. Not a lower bound and not a measured sector-wide average. Gas-derived CO2 captured in the design. Costs in paper $94–232/t reflect 2018 assumptions, not current market removal prices. Conflicts: author founder of Carbon Engineering. Use energy example with clear process boundary.

8. **SIPRI Yearbook 2026 press release**, **2026-06-08**. https://www.sipri.org/media/press-release/2026/increasing-focus-nuclear-weapons-amid-heightened-escalation-risks-new-sipri-yearbook-out-now . January 2026 estimate 12,187 total warheads, 9,745 military stockpiles, 4,012 deployed, 2,100–2,200 on high operational alert. Thus catastrophic destructive capacity already exists at order 10^4 weapons; AI need not invent new physics to interact dangerously with it. AI may improve verification or decision support and may compress decision times or create false confidence. Neither direction establishes a probability. “Nuclear weapons prevented war” is a counterfactual claim, not an experimentally established law.

9. **Xia et al., Nature Food**, 2022-08-15. https://www.nature.com/articles/s43016-022-00573-0 . Modeled nuclear soot/food scenarios estimate >2 billion deaths for modeled India–Pakistan exchange and >5 billion for large US–Russia exchange. These are conditional scenario outputs with major assumptions about soot, crop response, trade and adaptation, not forecasts or evidence for certain extinction. Use to show that food-system consequences can greatly exceed immediate blast harm, and that wars, climate disruption and resource access are correlated.

10. **WHO excess mortality**, 2022-05-05 release. https://www.who.int/news/item/05-05-2022-14.9-million-excess-deaths-were-associated-with-the-covid-19-pandemic-in-2020-and-2021 . Estimated 14.9 million deaths (13.3–16.6m) directly or indirectly associated with pandemic in 2020–2021. Order 10^7 deaths demonstrates major catastrophe with preserved global civilization. SI could accelerate surveillance, diagnostics and countermeasure discovery; effectiveness still needs validation, manufacturing, distribution, public trust. Biosecurity risk is dual-use; discuss at this high level, with no operational pathogen information.

11. **IEA Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026**, **2026-07-16**. https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026/executive-summary . Pipeline-based projected 2035 copper supply gap now 25% (30% in 2025 report); average leading refiner share excluding rare earths 72% in 2025 vs 70% 2023. A gap conditional on existing/announced projects and demand scenario is not inevitable depletion. Prices, substitution, recycling, new projects and demand changes can close it. SI can improve exploration/process design, but refining infrastructure, capital and geopolitical concentration remain. Resource scarcity is often a rate/location/power problem, not exhaustion of all atoms.

12. **International AI Safety Report 2026**, **2026-02-03**. https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026 . Sections 2.1.4 and 2.2.2. Scientific synthesis finds bio-assistance capabilities increasing but real-world effect uncertain; physical barriers and weak end-to-end evidence remain. Loss of control requires sufficient capability, harmful propensity, and enabling deployment. Present systems show early signs of relevant capabilities but not levels enabling loss of control as assessed by report. Expert likelihood judgments vary greatly. One can cite this for uncertainty and mechanism; do not present its existence as consensus on an extinction percentage. Keep this fourth/fifth risk channel: user's war/disease/climate/resources list otherwise omits an AI-specific mechanism.

## Original calculations for the expandable physics notebook

### Carbon cannot be removed by an idea alone

At E = 40GtCO2/year, replacing all annual emissions with DAC at Keith's specific energy requirement would consume:
- Gas input: 40e9 t/year *5.25e9 J/t = 2.1e20J/year = **210EJ/year**, average **6.7TW** gas-input power.
- Electricity: 40e9 *366kWh = **14,640TWh/year**, average **1.67TW** electricity.
- These are two input types; do not call the heat/gas figure electricity, and do not confuse them with facility nameplate power.
- This is a deliberately extreme scale exercise, not a proposed strategy. Avoided emissions should generally be pursued before building energy-intensive removal to undo them. Removal must be net of life-cycle emissions and stored durably.

A reversible ideal-gas minimum for separation at ambient CO2 mole fraction x=420ppm is roughly W_min = RT ln(1/x)/M_CO2. With R=8.314J/mol/K, T=298K, M=0.044kg/mol: **0.44GJ/t =0.12MWh/t**. At 40Gt/year, that's **~4,900TWh/year** even before real inefficiency, contactor pressure losses, compression and storage. This approximation concerns separating dilute ideal gas into pure CO2; it is not a universal lower bound for every removal chemistry (coupling to reactions changes accounting). Safer main-text calculation is the sourced process example above.

### Climate timing

At 40GtCO2/year, waiting 10 years adds order 400GtCO2. Using IPCC central TCRE gives ~0.18°C CO2-induced warming; 20 years ~0.36°C. Both are conditional constant-emissions calculations, excluding changes in other forcings and feedbacks beyond TCRE assessment. SI's relevant metric is accumulated emissions avoided before buildout, not number of elegant climate plans generated.

## Historical framing

Technologies can amplify war without causing war. Nuclear fission simultaneously enabled electricity, medicine and unprecedented weapons; it was developed for wartime deployment, not proof that civilian technology automatically causes conflict. For a direct historical source if needed: US National Park Service, Manhattan Project https://www.nps.gov/mapr/learn/manhattan-project.htm (Trinity 1945-07-16, Hiroshima 1945-08-06). Counterexamples and counterfactuals matter: nuclear deterrence might avert some wars while creating rare catastrophic tail risk. The observable arsenal is stronger evidence than confident claims about either counterfactual.

## Jancovici caveat worth retaining in research notes

His 2008 article https://jancovici.com/en/climate-change/predicting-the-future/will-climate-change-get-rapidely-to-a-halt-if-we-quickly-decrease-the-emissions/ uses an older picture to discuss continuing impacts after emissions peak and includes a “CO2 remains a century” simplification. Do not quote this as current precise carbon-cycle science. Emissions merely decreasing is different from reaching net zero. Modern IPCC supports roughly stabilizing CO2-induced surface temperature around net zero, with uncertainty and non-CO2 caveats; sea-level rise continues. His physical-stock/flow emphasis is useful, but every dated number still needs verification.

## Evidence that should move the living probabilities

Downward doom updates: independently demonstrated reliable oversight under adversarial testing; actual reductions in nuclear decision automation risk; lower verified real-world misuse capability at fixed beneficial capability; substantially faster surveillance-to-effective-countermeasure deployment; sustained falling net emissions and resilient energy/food systems; demonstrated diverse refining and recycling output. Upward updates: autonomous evasion/persistence that defeats credible containment; actual harmful deployment at scale; erosion of crisis communication and arms control; real-world misuse uplift; sustained emissions increases or shrinking climate/adaptation capacity; concentration and export shocks that cause demonstrated cascading failures.

A benchmark improvement alone can raise P(SI), increase P(abundance|SI), and also increase P(doom|SI). Separate capability evidence from evidence about control and deployment. Do not automatically map every exciting headline to optimism.
