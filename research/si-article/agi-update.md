## Definitions to add before the SI definition

A workable **AGI** definition is a system able to learn and carry out a broad range of unfamiliar cognitive tasks at roughly competent-human performance, across domains and tools, with stated resource limits. This is broader than a specialist and less demanding than **SI**, which reliably exceeds strong expert teams. There is no agreed scalar AGI threshold. Some reasonable definitions may already be met; definitions requiring dependable autonomous work across essentially all relevant environments, or broad physical competence, remain stronger claims. “Near AGI” can therefore be a defensible interpretation of evidence, not an AA certification. State which level the author means.

Keep separate: cognitive generality; expert-level frontier performance; reliable long-horizon autonomy; real-world embodiment; and scalable deployment. Requiring physical dexterity in the AGI definition is optional and must not be silently added merely to move the goalposts. SI as defined in the draft also need not personally execute each robot motion; scientific planning plus specialist controllers may be the relevant system.

## Comparability rule

AA v5 launched27Oct;v4.1.1/v4.3.2 figures from September are a DIFFERENT measuring instrument. Use [AA Progress Index v1 methodology](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/progress-index/) for longitudinal claims: it links shared tasks using item response theory. It remains a statistical measurement model, not an absolute intelligence scale. The methodology explicitly says Intelligence Index versions are not intended for long-run comparisons. For updates store date,modelvariant,effort,harness,benchmarkversion,cost,score,availability,status. Do not read Astra61.2(v4.1.1 at launch)→57(v5 today) as cognitive decline.

AA's benchmark/model pages sometimes show generic “No publicly available API” placeholders in chart text even while populated model summaries give prices and throughput. Use provider model docs/release statements for product availability. Astra's API existence is independently confirmed by official model docs: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-astra . Opus5.5's official docs: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/opus-5-5/overview . No AGI certification found on AA pages. Interpret the evidence;do not attribute “AGI”to AA.

## Two suggested article paragraphs

**The case for expecting SI should begin with what has already happened. GPT-6 Astra and Opus5.5 operate across coding,scientific workflows,documents,images and computer interfaces;they are no longer narrow task-specific systems. Artificial Analysis's late-October evaluations find strong performance across independently tested research and software tasks,with private tests increasingly replacing saturated benchmarks. ARC Prize's results show Astra learning unfamiliar interactive environments,and recent robotics studies show it transferring those capabilities into planning and numerical control. It is reasonable to regard this as substantial progress toward AGI:generality around human-level work. The remaining step to SI is more demanding—reliable performance beyond strong expert teams—but it now has an empirical starting point.** [AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index-v5),[ARC](https://arcprize.org/results/openai-gpt-6),[robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22471).

**The residual uncertainty is increasingly about the performance envelope. How reliable is the system on unfamiliar work,how much does a successful project cost,and how much of the real world can it control on a clock that does not pause? A model can dominate a difficult benchmark while still fail a large fraction of new software changes;an impressive simulated robot can deliberate for hours over seconds of motion. Meanwhile,the cheaper Sol and Terra models show that stronger capability can spread without everyone paying frontier prices. This combination makes a high probability of SI defensible as a forecast. It does not make the forecast certain,orturn a benchmark score into its probability.** [AA model cost](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gpt-6-1-sol),[Galbot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38537).
