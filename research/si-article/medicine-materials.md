# Medicine and materials for abundance

Evidence checked 1 November 2026. This memo adds mechanisms and physical/statistical scales. It does not assign new numerical outcome probabilities. It uses exactly three research questions; the suggested publication text is under the final question. Current source pages can change, so labels below preserve the dated observation.

## Which medical technologies can SI accelerate, and what evidence must connect prediction to health?

### Takeaway

Medicine has several distinct production chains. SI can improve every stage, including experimental design and manufacturing, but success must reach patients as a net clinical benefit. Essential-health abundance can improve greatly through delivery of existing prevention and treatments, without curing every disease or proving radical longevity.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences

- The working causal chain is **disease mechanism → intervention → delivery to the relevant cells → sufficient exposure → desired biological change → meaningful benefit exceeding harms → affordable delivery at population scale**. Sequence/structure/binding predictions address parts of the chain. A cellular result, a biomarker change and a patient outcome are different observations, with different external validity. This is a synthesis of the trial, label and surrogate framework cited above.
- Better models can reduce false candidates and choose more informative experiments. They can also create better molecules or alternative process architectures. It is too rigid to hold today's discovery/approval durations fixed under SI; the six-month individualized editing case demonstrates that compressed development can occur in a well-defined setting. It is equally unjustified to extrapolate that result to every disease or to late adverse effects. [NIH case](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/infant-rare-incurable-disease-first-successfully-receive-personalized-gene-therapy-treatment).
- A usable healthcare basket should specify prevention, diagnosis and treatment coverage, rather than merely count new drugs. The WHO essential-medicines framework offers a defensible starting point. [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/essential-medicines).

### Gaps

- This bounded review did not establish representative joules, labour hours, cost or capacity per successful treatment across modalities. These are heterogeneous and cannot be inferred from protein-design benchmark scores.
- No calibrated probability follows for broad clinical success by2050. That requires disease-specific baseline burden, attainable coverage, efficacy and delivery scenarios.
- Nature full-text fetches for rentosertib and some materials papers encountered publisher redirects. Their indexed official abstracts were available; NIH/FDA/WHO and NYU pages were read directly. The NEJM islet full-text fetch returned403, so only its indexed original summary is used. NIA's ITP page was indexed but its direct response required a robot check; do not imply full-page review.

## What quantitative tests make clinical and manufacturing bottlenecks explicit?

### Takeaway

Information requirements and production architecture matter as much as laboratory discovery speed. These simple calculations expose the scaling without claiming that current trial designs or factories are immutable.

### Cited Findings

- NIH's study-quality framework emphasizes prespecified outcomes, adequate statistical power and randomization. Its conventional reference is at least80% power for a clinically relevant difference with two-sided alpha=.05. NIH methods resources also require stated effect sizes, follow-up, missingness and multiplicity assumptions. [NHLBI](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health-topics/study-quality-assessment-tools); [NIH methods](https://researchmethodsresources.nih.gov/methods/additional).
- FDA recognizes surrogate endpoints that predict clinical benefit, with context-dependent acceptability. A surrogate appropriate for one drug, disease or patient population need not transfer to another. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-resources/table-surrogate-endpoints-were-basis-drug-approval-or-licensure).
- FDA's2024 genome-editing webinar recommends long-term follow-up for up to15years because long-term on/off-target effects may be unknown. This is follow-up after administration, **not a universal requirement to wait15years before approval or treating another patient**. [FDA transcript](https://www.fda.gov/media/177579/download).
- Casgevy's minimum recommended dose is3×10^6CD34+cells/kg, with each product linked to an individual patient's identity; collection and conditioning precede infusion. [FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/media/174615/download).

### Inferences

**1. Small effects require more observations.** For two independent, equal-sized groups with a continuous endpoint, equal varianceσ², normal approximation, two-sided5% significance and80% power:

`n per arm ≈ 2(z_.975 + z_.80)^2 σ²/Δ² ≈ 15.7/d²`, where `d=Δ/σ`.

At d=.5, n≈63per arm; at d=.1, n≈1,568per arm. Five times smaller standardized effect needs25times more participants. These are illustrative calculations, not trial recommendations: binary/survival endpoints, clustering, missing data, multiplicity and uncertain variance change the result. Better endpoints, stratification, repeated measures and better treatments can reduce the burden; randomization and validation still matter. Methodological relevance: [NIH](https://researchmethodsresources.nih.gov/methods/additional).

**2. Rare harm and efficacy have different sample needs.** If each independently exposed patient has event probability p, the chance of seeing at least one after n exposures is `1−(1−p)^n`. For p=10^-4,95% detection requires `n≥ln(.05)/ln(.9999)≈29,956`, about30,000 exposures. This does not prove the event was caused by treatment, establish acceptable risk, or detect a harm that has not yet had time to appear. It explains why a71-patient,12-week trial leaves large safety/durability uncertainties. Trial context: [rentosertib](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03743-2).

**3. Patient-specific production has a queue.** Let annual treatments T=10million and assume each individualized batch occupies a validated production position for14days. Required concurrent positions are `T×14/365≈384,000`. These inputs are explicit author scenarios, not measured Casgevy cycle times or a prediction of demand. The simple Little's-law-style throughput calculation excludes failures, downtime and reserves. A shorter validated process, more production modules, pooled allogeneic cells or a successful in-vivo treatment could transform the architecture. The reason to consider individual identity/collection constraints is established by the [FDA Casgevy label](https://www.fda.gov/media/174615/download). At its minimum dose, a70-kg recipient needs at least2.1×10^8cells; a million such treatments require2.1×10^14qualified cells. Aggregate cell count alone hides individualized quality/identity constraints.

**4. Faster design can leave the rest of the chain dominant.** Under a simple sequential model, if fraction f of elapsed time is accelerated r-fold, speedup is `1/[(1−f)+f/r]`. f=.2,r=100 gives1.25×; f=.8 gives4.81×. This is an accounting identity under stated assumptions, not a law fixing today's stages. Parallel trials, better surrogates, reusable manufacturing platforms and redesigned delivery can change f itself. FDA's context-specific surrogate framework and the personalized-editing case demonstrate why both immovable-delay pessimism and instant-cure extrapolation are too strong. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-resources/table-surrogate-endpoints-were-basis-drug-approval-or-licensure); [NIH](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/infant-rare-incurable-disease-first-successfully-receive-personalized-gene-therapy-treatment).

### Gaps

- The14-day occupancy and10million/year are sensitivity inputs. Do not publish them as current industrial performance.
- Clinical endpoints and delivery needs vary greatly. A generic count of 'medical discoveries' cannot determine the health gateH.
- Mechanistic models could replace some experiments if prospectively validated, but their predictive performance for new interventions remains an empirical question.

## Which materials and process advances could move several constraints at once?

### Takeaway

Catalysts, feedstock changes and better materials can redesign the industrial path. They can reduce cost and waste while leaving mass balance, energy balance, qualification and manufacturing throughput intact.

### Cited Findings

- DOE's current demonstration portfolio includes calcined-clay cement to reduce clinker, silicate-based alternatives, electrochemical production and carbon capture. These are distinct development routes; DOE describes the facilities as planned/demonstration projects rather than a proven replacement of the global fleet. [DOE cement portfolio](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/oced/industrial-demonstrations-program-selected-and-awarded-projects-cement-and-concrete).
- A2025 original study reports electrolytic extraction of calcium from waste cement to produce clinker precursor, with experimental emissions reductions depending on fresh versus carbonated waste feed. This is process evidence; its laboratory percentages are not a global life-cycle result. [Original research](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64339-5).
- A DOE-hosted2024 hydrogen-infrastructure workshop uses55kWh/kgH₂ as a PEM example and emphasizes steady-state ironmaking supplied by storage/pipelines. Its practical example uses80–120kgH₂/tonne metallic iron, above the chemical stoichiometric minimum calculated below. [DOE-hosted workshop](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-02/h2-infrastructure-strategies-workshop-hammond.pdf).
- University of Tokyo researchers report strained nickelate thin films near40K at ambient pressure and67–73K at16GPa in2026. These are research advances, with material form, pressure and critical-current engineering still relevant; neither result supplies room-temperature grid cable. [Research institution and paper reference](https://www.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/press/pr2026-08-20-002).

### Inferences

**Cement: clean heat alone leaves process carbon.** `CaCO3 → CaO + CO2`: producing56gCaO releases44gCO₂ if carbonate is the source. With an illustrative clinker containing65%CaO derived from limestone, the process release is `(44/56)×.65≈.51tonnesCO₂/tonneclinker`, before fuel emissions. Producing one billion tonnes of this clinker implies roughly0.5Gt process CO₂. SI could optimize substitutes, durability, structural material use, separations and capture, or develop alternative feeds/processes. It cannot remove carbon atoms from this reaction by improving the kiln controller. DOE's active alternatives show that the reaction path itself can change. [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/oced/industrial-demonstrations-program-selected-and-awarded-projects-cement-and-concrete); [clinker accounting protocol](https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/2023-03/co2_CSI_Cement_Protocol-V2.0_0.pdf).

**Iron: a reductant still needs to be supplied.** `Fe2O3 + 3H2 → 2Fe + 3H2O` requires `3×2.016/(2×55.845)≈.0541tonnesH₂/tonneFe`:54kgH₂/tFe under perfect stoichiometry. At assumed55kWh/kgH₂, that is about3MWh/tFe just for electrolytic hydrogen, excluding actual excess/recycle, ore preparation, heating and the electric furnace. Applying that illustrative lower process quantity to2GtFe/year requires about6,000TWh/year. This is a hypothetical scale test, not today's all-steel route or a minimum across all ironmaking technologies. Better catalysts and control can improve kinetics/yield; electrochemical iron and more scrap recycling can change the process. Source context: [DOE-hosted workshop](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-02/h2-infrastructure-strategies-workshop-hammond.pdf).

**Superconductors: useful wire is the unit.** Better superconductors could improve magnets, motors and selected networks. Candidate discovery must become long conductors with sufficient critical current and field tolerance, economical fabrication, mechanical reliability and manageable cooling. SI could accelerate that full materials pipeline. A higher transition temperature in a tiny film is only one condition. The current nickelate evidence illustrates the distinction. [Tokyo research](https://www.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/press/pr2026-08-20-002).

**Three neglected developments worth including in the broad thesis:**

1. **Reusable delivery platforms.** Lipid particles, tissue-targeting ligands, safe in-vivo editing and modular process validation could change gene therapy from an individualized factory operation into a repeatable product family. This moves the manufacturing architecture and thereforeH, rather than merely accelerating candidate generation. Liver-directed personalized editing establishes an early route, not universal delivery. [NIH case](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/infant-rare-incurable-disease-first-successfully-receive-personalized-gene-therapy-treatment).
2. **Immune-compatible replacement cells.** A scalable, durable cell source without lifelong generalized immunosuppression could improve both organ supply and delivery cost. Early engineered-islet evidence is far below proof of population-scale success; it identifies a concrete variable to test: years of organ function per qualified batch. [NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2503822).
3. **Process redesign using alternative feedstocks.** Low-clinker/silicate/electrochemical cement or low-carbon iron routes can change which resource is binding. New intelligence should be allowed to change a process, not forced to optimize today's machinery forever. Conversely, new methods need durability, net-emissions and cost evidence at production scale. [DOE cement](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/oced/industrial-demonstrations-program-selected-and-awarded-projects-cement-and-concrete).

**Suggested visible article passage (roughly650words; adapt and avoid duplicating existing clinical notes):**

Medicine has a longer causal chain than protein prediction. A target must matter to disease. A treatment must reach it, change the biology, improve patients' lives and remain safer than its alternatives. It must then be made and delivered. SI could improve every link. No single benchmark measures the chain.

The routes already exist. Generative chemistry and protein design expand the candidate space. mRNA platforms reuse manufacturing knowledge across products. Gene editing can correct particular defects. Engineered cells and animal organs may expand replacement supply. Diagnostics and prevention can improve health without inventing another drug. Their evidence ranges from laboratory function to randomized trials and approved treatments; the maturity labels matter. [Trial](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03743-2), [WHO platforms](https://www.who.int/initiatives/mrna-technology-transfer-%28mrna-tt%29-programme), [FDA gene therapy](https://www.fda.gov/media/174615/download), [NYU organ trial](https://nyulangone.org/news/first-gene-edited-pig-kidney-transplant-clinical-trial-begins-nyu-langone-health).

The statistical scale is explicit. In a simplified two-arm continuous-outcome trial,80%power and5%two-sided significance require about15.7/d²participants per arm, where d is the treatment effect divided by the outcome's standard deviation. d=.5 needs about63; d=.1 needs about1,600. A rarer problem needs another test: a harm occurring once per10,000independent exposures needs roughly30,000exposures for a95%chance of observing it at least once. Neither calculation establishes causation or catches a delayed harm before it appears. Better measurements and stronger treatments can reduce the information burden. [NIH trial methods](https://researchmethodsresources.nih.gov/methods/additional).

Today's timelines are not immutable. A personalized liver-editing therapy went from diagnosis to treatment in six months. Validated surrogate endpoints can shorten some trials. Yet a changed biomarker must predict meaningful benefit in the intended setting. Long-term surveillance for genome editing can continue for up to15years after administration; that is not a15-year preapproval waiting rule. [NIH case](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/infant-rare-incurable-disease-first-successfully-receive-personalized-gene-therapy-treatment), [FDA surrogates](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-resources/table-surrogate-endpoints-were-basis-drug-approval-or-licensure), [FDA follow-up](https://www.fda.gov/media/177579/download).

Manufacturing architecture can matter more than molecular-design speed. Suppose10million individualized treatments per year each occupy a production position for14days. About384,000positions must run concurrently, before failures and downtime. These are scenario assumptions. Shorter validated processes, off-the-shelf cells or in-vivo treatment could change the requirement. Casgevy's actual collection, individualized identity and conditioning requirements explain why a successful edited cell is not yet universal access. [FDA label](https://www.fda.gov/media/174615/download).

Materials offer similar opportunities. Limestone releases44gCO₂ for every56gCaO made by calcination. Illustrative65%-CaO clinker therefore emits about0.5tonnes process CO₂ per tonne even with clean heat. Low-clinker cements, silicate feeds, electrochemical processes and capture can change that path. Hydrogen reduction of iron similarly needs at least54kgH₂/tFe by stoichiometry; at assumed55kWh/kg, hydrogen alone consumes about3MWh/tFe. Better catalysts improve rates and selectivity. Different processes can move the constraint itself. [DOE cement](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/oced/industrial-demonstrations-program-selected-and-awarded-projects-cement-and-concrete), [DOE iron example](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-02/h2-infrastructure-strategies-workshop-hammond.pdf).

For abundance, count durable health gained, qualified treatments delivered and useful material services per unit cost. Existing vaccines still miss millions of children. Discovery, delivery and access all belong in the probability model. [WHO coverage](https://www.who.int/en/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/immunization-coverage).

### Gaps

- The cement/iron numerical exercises use specified feedstock routes. Alternative chemistries can evade a route-specific bound; they cannot evade conservation of matter or energy.
- The exact global share of future cement/steel served by these routes is not estimated here. Pilot success does not identify2050 market share or adoption probability.
- The suggested visible passage is an editorial draft, not a separate fourth research question. Publication should link to detailed assumptions if it keeps the statistical or manufacturing scenarios.
