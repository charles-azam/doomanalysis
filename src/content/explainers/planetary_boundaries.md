## Why it matters here

This is a plain measure with no danger band. Each boundary marks how far a process, such as the climate or the nitrogen cycle, can be pushed while the risk of destabilizing the planet stays low ([Planetary Health Check 2026](https://publications.pik-potsdam.de/pubman/item/item_35009/component/file_35022/PlanetaryHealthCheck2026.pdf)). Crossing one is a warning, not a disaster. The harm itself is counted on other cards, such as warming, species loss and fresh water.

## In numbers

- **The count has risen:** four boundaries were crossed in the [2015 update](https://www.stockholmresilience.org/research/planetary-boundaries.html), six in [2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10499318/) and seven in [2025](https://www.stockholmresilience.org/news--events/general-news/2025-09-24-seven-of-nine-planetary-boundaries-now-breached), when ocean acidification joined. The [2026 check](https://www.planetaryhealthcheck.org/) still finds seven, [all getting worse](https://publications.pik-potsdam.de/pubman/item/item_35009/component/file_35022/PlanetaryHealthCheck2026.pdf).
- **Climate:** CO₂ is at 426 ppm (parts per million), against a boundary of 350 ([PHC 2026](https://publications.pik-potsdam.de/pubman/item/item_35009/component/file_35022/PlanetaryHealthCheck2026.pdf)). The lowest emissions scenario still leaves [393 ppm in 2100](https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/13/3571/2020/), so this boundary stays crossed.
- **Life:** species go extinct at over 100 per million species per year, against 10, and people take 21 to 30% of the energy plants capture, against 10% ([PHC 2026](https://publications.pik-potsdam.de/pubman/item/item_35009/component/file_35022/PlanetaryHealthCheck2026.pdf)).
- **Still safe:** the ozone layer, back inside its limit thanks to international controls on ozone-destroying chemicals, though recovery may have slowed, and aerosols (airborne particles), safe worldwide but over the regional limit in South Asia ([PHC 2026](https://publications.pik-potsdam.de/pubman/item/item_35009/component/file_35022/PlanetaryHealthCheck2026.pdf)).

## The lines on the card

- **4:** the count in the 2015 update.
- **6:** the count in 2023, the first update to measure all nine ([Stockholm Resilience Centre](https://www.stockholmresilience.org/research/planetary-boundaries.html)).
- **7:** the count in 2025 and 2026.
- **8:** only one boundary left inside its limit.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

No study projects the count to 2100, so the judges estimate it. Falling below seven needs one of the crossed processes to recover, and climate cannot by 2100 in any scenario. Rising to eight needs the ozone layer or aerosols to fail again. The count also hides depth: a boundary barely crossed counts the same as one deep in the high-risk zone.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Cheaper clean energy and substitutes for fertilizer and persistent chemicals could ease several boundaries. Most are stocks, such as CO₂ in the air or species lost, that recover over decades to centuries whatever is invented.
