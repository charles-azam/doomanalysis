## Why it matters here

An overfished stock yields less than it could, so the catch shrinks. The harm falls on poor coastal people who get protein and micronutrients (vitamins and minerals such as zinc and iron) from fish and have [few alternatives](https://www.washington.edu/news/2016/06/15/falling-fish-catches-could-mean-malnutrition-in-the-developing-world/). Fish farms now [produce more](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/06/1167727) than the wild catch, which keeps this card at level 2 or below.

## In numbers

- **Today:** [37.6% of assessed stocks](https://news.mongabay.com/2026/06/aquatic-animal-and-terrestrial-meat-trades-now-almost-on-par-fao-report-finds/) were overfished in [2023](https://www.fao.org/publications/sofia/2026), against [10% in 1974](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-of-fish-stocks-overexploited).
- **Catch:** the wild catch has stayed between [86 and 94 million tonnes](https://www.msc.org/media-centre/news-opinion/news/2026/06/19/five-takeaways-from-the-fao-sofia-report-2026) since the late 1980s, and was [92 million in 2024](https://news.mongabay.com/2026/06/aquatic-animal-and-terrestrial-meat-trades-now-almost-on-par-fao-report-finds/). 72.6% of assessed landings come from healthy stocks, so roughly (1 − 0.726) × 92 = 25 million tonnes come from overfished ones.
- **Farms:** aquaculture reached [103.3 million tonnes](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/06/1167727) in 2024.
- **Who relies on it:** aquatic foods give [3.1 billion people](https://www.msc.org/media-centre/news-opinion/news/2026/06/19/five-takeaways-from-the-fao-sofia-report-2026) at least a fifth of their animal protein.

## What each level looks like

- **0 to 35%, level 1.** Our judgment: stocks rebuild and harm stays local.
- **35 to 60%, level 1.** Our judgment: as today, the catch is flat while farms and trade fill the gap. In the Mediterranean and Black Sea, only [58 of 127 stocks](https://www.msc.org/media-centre/news-opinion/news/2026/06/19/five-takeaways-from-the-fao-sofia-report-2026) are healthy, so 69 ÷ 127 = 54.3% are already overfished.
- **60 to 100%, level 2.** Our judgment: the wild catch falls. [845 million people](https://www.washington.edu/news/2016/06/15/falling-fish-catches-could-mean-malnutrition-in-the-developing-world/), 11% of humanity, are vulnerable to micronutrient deficiencies as catches decline. Only part of them would lose a decent diet, since farmed fish and other foods can replace some of it, so we stay below 1 in 10.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

No source projects the overfished share to 2100, so the judges estimate it. The FAO series rose from [10% in 1974 to 35.5% in 2021](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-of-fish-stocks-overexploited). FAO also warns that [rising temperatures](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/06/1167727) threaten fisheries, a harm this measure does not count.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Satellite tracking and better stock models could expose illegal fishing and set smarter quotas, and fish farming could grow. Stocks rebuild only when boats catch less, which is a matter of enforcement and politics.
