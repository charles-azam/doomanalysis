## Why it matters here

The number of conflicts says how widespread fighting is. Battle deaths say how intense it is: one large war between states can kill more than fifty small conflicts. The count covers [soldiers and civilians killed in the fighting](https://www.uu.se/en/department/peace-and-conflict-research/research/ucdp/ucdp-definitions), not those who die of the hunger and disease war brings. So this card has no danger levels; the full toll is read on the top card.

## In numbers

- In 2025, fighting that involved a government killed about [153,600 people](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/deaths-in-state-based-conflicts-by-type). Among 8.2 billion people, that is [1.9 per 100,000](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/death-rate-in-state-based-conflicts).
- The war between Russia and Ukraine caused at least 94,700 of these deaths, or [62%](https://www.uu.se/en/press/press-releases/2026/2026-06-09-ucdp-record-number-of-conflicts-between-states).
- Today's rate is 10 times the rate of 2005 (1.9 / 0.19) and 12 times lower than the rate of 1950 (22 / 1.9).
- The Second World War caused [16.6 million battle deaths](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5834001/) in six years, among [2.3 billion people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II_casualties). 16.6 million / 6 / 2.3 billion = about 120 per 100,000 a year.

## The lines on the card

- **0.19, in 2005.** The calmest year since the series begins in 1946, with about 12,400 deaths.
- **3.6, in 2022.** The highest rate since 1987.
- **22, in 1950.** The first year of the Korean War and the highest rate in the series, with about 550,000 deaths.
- **120, the Second World War.** The yearly average computed above. It is over 60 times today's rate.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

Nobody publishes a forecast for 2100. The value in any one year depends mostly on whether a large war is under way. [Clauset 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5834001/) finds no trend in the size of wars between states from 1823 to 2003. Only if the calm among great powers since 1945 holds for 100 to 150 more years could it be told apart from luck. The low rates of the 2000s are no promise for 2100.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Precise autonomous weapons could spare soldiers and civilians. They could also let wars be fought on a larger scale for less money. Better medicine saves more of the wounded. The decision to fight stays human and political.
