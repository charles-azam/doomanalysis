## Why it matters here

Hunger, unsafe water, missing clinics and violence all end up as early deaths, so life expectancy is the broadest single test of whether the essentials work. It describes this year's death rates, not any real person's lifespan.

## In numbers

- **Today.** The world reached 73.5 years in 2024, up from 65.1 in 1990 ([World Bank](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/WLD;HIC/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.IN?format=json&per_page=200&date=1990:2025)). The UN, with its own estimate of 73.3, counts 8.4 years gained since 1995 ([UN](https://population.un.org/wpp/assets/Files/WPP2024_Summary-of-Results.pdf)).
- **Shocks.** COVID-19 pulled the world from 72.9 in 2019 to 71.2 in 2021; by 2024 it was above its old level ([World Bank](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/WLD;HIC/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.IN?format=json&per_page=200&date=1990:2025)). A one-year shock shows at once, even when most lifespans barely change.
- **Rich and best.** The rich world is at 80.4 ([World Bank](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/WLD;HIC/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.IN?format=json&per_page=200&date=1990:2025)). Hong Kong, at 85.4, is the highest of any large population; only tiny Monaco and San Marino are higher, at 86.5 and 85.8 ([World Bank](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.IN?format=json&date=2024&per_page=400)).
- **Pace.** From 65.1 to 73.5 in 34 years is 0.25 years a year. The UN expects 77.4 by 2054 ([UN](https://population.un.org/wpp/assets/Files/WPP2024_Summary-of-Results.pdf)), and IHME 78.1 by 2050 ([ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2024/05/240517164149.htm)).

## The lines on the card

- **65.1 years:** the world in 1990.
- **80.4 years:** the rich world today.
- **85.4 years:** Hong Kong today, the highest large population.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

The UN's probabilistic projections give the world 81.7 years in 2100, with an 80% range of 80.1 to 83.1 and a 95% range of 79.0 to 83.9 ([UN WPP 2024](https://population.un.org/wpp/assets/Excel%20Files/2_Indicators%20(Probabilistic)/EXCEL_FILES/4_Mortality/UN_PPP2024_Output_e0.xlsx)). Those five numbers are the published points. The range is narrow because it is built from past national trends; in our reading it does not allow for a large war, famine or pandemic, or for a breakthrough against aging. The judges fill in those tails.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Faster discovery of drugs and vaccines could add years, and slowing aging would add many more. Trials, regulation and health workers set the pace, and the same tools could help design pathogens.
