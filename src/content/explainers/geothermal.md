## Why it matters here

The Earth's heat gives steady power day and night. Classic plants need hot water near the surface. Enhanced wells pump fluid into hot rock at high pressure to open cracks it can flow through ([REN21](https://ren21.net/gsr-2025/technologies/geothermal)). The IEA says such methods could in principle supply many times the world's demand for electricity and heat ([Energy Global](https://www.energyglobal.com/other-renewables/23122024/iea-technology-breakthroughs-are-unlocking-geothermal-energys-vast-potential-in-countries-across-the-globe)). If drilling gets cheap, this card can reach level 3.

## In numbers

- Geothermal plants made about 99 TWh in 2024 from 15.1 GW ([REN21](https://ren21.net/gsr-2025/technologies/geothermal)). The world made about 2,131 / 0.069 ≈ 30,900 TWh that year, solar's output over its share ([Ember](https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2025/2024-in-review/)), so geothermal's share was 99 / 30,900 ≈ 0.3%. The IEA's "about 1%" is rounded up; [E&E News](https://www.eenews.net/articles/geothermal-could-meet-15-of-global-power-demand-report/) says under 1%. Only about 1 GW net was added in 2019–2024 (REN21).
- The IEA sees up to 800 GW by 2050 if next-generation costs fall 80% by 2035, to about $50 per MWh (Energy Global). At the 88% capacity factor of plants built in 2024 ([IRENA](https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Publication/2025/Jul/IRENA_TEC_RPGC_in_2024_Summary_2025.pdf)), that is 800 × 8,760 × 0.88 ≈ 6,200 TWh a year, a fifth of today's world electricity.
- Fervo's enhanced plant at Cape Station, Utah, began commercial operation in October 2026 with a 33 MW block; 100 MW is due by January 2027 and 400 MW more in 2028 ([Energy Global](https://www.energyglobal.com/other-renewables/05102026/fervo-energy-declares-commercial-operation-at-cape-station-ahead-of-schedule/)). Eavor's closed-loop wells in Germany first sent power to the grid in December 2025 ([POWER](https://www.powermag.com/eavors-first-of-its-kind-closed-loop-geothermal-project-produces-grid-power-in-germany/)).

## What each level looks like

- **0.1 to 1%, level 1.** Our judgment: power only where hot water lies near the surface.
- **1 to 10%, level 1.** Our judgment: steady power in many countries that other clean sources could also supply, like fission today.
- **10 to 30%, level 2.** Our judgment: steady power for hundreds of millions.
- **30 to 50%, level 3.** Our judgment: deep heat tapped almost everywhere, for billions.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

Nothing is published for 2100. The IEA's 800 GW is a 2050 potential, not a forecast.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Suppose superintelligence arrives in the 2030s. It could read rock from seismic data, steer drills and run reservoirs, and up to 80% of the investment draws on oil and gas skills, the IEA says (Energy Global). Wells and plants still take years: Fervo took 23 months to build its first block and aims for 18.
