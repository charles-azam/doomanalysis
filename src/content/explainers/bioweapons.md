## Why it matters here

A germ that passes from person to person does not stop at a border, so one attack could in principle kill far more than any bomb. So far none has. This card counts attacks by states and by groups, and reads its levels like the top card, by the share killed. It overlaps with the card on human-caused pandemics, which counts the same releases plus laboratory accidents.

## In numbers

- In the 20th century, deliberate releases killed [several tens of thousands](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1326439/), most of them by Japan's army in China in the Second World War. Taking 50,000 against the [2.3 billion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II_casualties) then alive gives 0.002%. The 2001 anthrax letters in the United States killed five.
- The [XPT report](https://forecastingresearch.org/pdf/existential-risk-persuasion-tournament.pdf) knows of no biological attack that killed over 100,000 people, 0.0012% of today's [8.2 billion](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL). For contagious attacks that kill that many by 2050, its median superforecaster expected 0.15 by states and 0.04 by groups; the median biosecurity expert, none.

## What each level looks like

- **Under 1%, level 1.** Up to 80 million dead; every attack so far sits near the bottom. For scale, COVID-19 caused about [27 million](https://ourworldindata.org/historical-pandemics) excess deaths, some 0.3%.
- **1 to 10%, level 2.** 80 to 800 million dead (the share times 8.2 billion): a deliberate pandemic.
- **10 to 50%, level 3.** 0.8 to 4 billion dead from one release.
- **50 to 99%, level 4, and over 99%, level 5.** Our judgment, as on the top card, since no study models an attack this large: with under 1% left, survivors are too few to rebuild within a century.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

No forecast isolates deliberate attacks up to 2100. The closest is the XPT question on genetically engineered pathogens. Its superforecasters gave 4% for one killing over 1% of humanity by 2100, 0.8% for over 10%, and 0.01% for extinction; its biorisk experts 10.25%, 3% and 1%. We use the superforecasters as a stand-in. Their figures include accidents, which makes them too high, and leave out attacks with unmodified germs, which makes them too low.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Faster detection, vaccines and treatments would limit how far an attack spreads. The same tools could lower the skill needed to cause harm. Defences still need factories, trials and public trust.
