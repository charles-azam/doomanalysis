## Why it matters here

Northern permafrost holds [1,330 to 1,580 billion tonnes of carbon](https://www.carbonbrief.org/guest-post-what-the-latest-science-says-about-thawing-permafrost). The air holds [423.9 ppm of CO₂ at 7.8 billion t per ppm](https://library.wmo.int/records/item/69807-state-of-the-global-climate-2025): 3,306 billion t of CO₂, or 3,306 × 12 ÷ 44 ≈ 900 billion t of carbon, so the frozen ground holds 1.5 to 1.8 times as much. Thawing soils release it as CO₂ and methane, a loss [irreversible for centuries](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_SPM.pdf). It harms people through extra warming, which keeps it at level 1, or just into level 2 above 250 billion t.

## In numbers

- **So far, about 1 billion t.** Over 2000–2020 the permafrost region emitted a net [38 million t of carbon a year as methane and 12 million t as CO₂, whose sign is uncertain](https://doi.org/10.1029/2023GB007953): 0.05 billion t × 21 years ≈ 1.
- **By 2100:** [about 55 billion t if warming is held to 2 °C, 232 billion t with no climate policy](https://in.nau.edu/news/country-of-permafrost/). IPCC models give [3 to 41 billion t per °C but leave out abrupt thaw](https://www.woodwellclimate.org/review-of-permafrost-science-in-ipccs-ar6-wg1/), the slumping of ice-rich ground, which [adds about half again](https://pik-potsdam.de/members/johanro/ArmstrongMcKayetal2022_ClimateTippingPointsReassessment_AcceptedversionwithFigures.pdf).
- **Heat per tonne.** A collapse releasing [125–250 billion t adds 0.2–0.4 °C](https://pik-potsdam.de/members/johanro/ArmstrongMcKayetal2022_ClimateTippingPointsReassessment_AcceptedversionwithFigures.pdf): 0.2 ÷ 125 = 0.0016 °C per billion t.
- **People per degree.** Each extra 0.1 °C puts [about 140 million more people in dangerous heat](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2023/05/230522131306.htm), a yearly average of 29 °C or more. Like the warming card, we count one in five of them as losing decent living: 28 million per 0.1 °C. One percent of the [10.2 billion people of 2100](https://www.dawn.com/news/1845492) is 102 million.

## What each level looks like

- **0–125 billion t, level 1.** Up to 125 × 0.0016 = 0.2 °C, so up to 2 × 28 = 56 million people, 0.5%.
- **125–250 billion t, level 1.** The collapse range, whose [central threshold is 4 °C (3–6)](https://pik-potsdam.de/members/johanro/ArmstrongMcKayetal2022_ClimateTippingPointsReassessment_AcceptedversionwithFigures.pdf): 0.2–0.4 °C, 56 to 112 million people, 0.5–1.1%.
- **250–300 billion t, level 2.** Up to 0.48 °C: 112 to 134 million people, just over 1%.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

No source gives probabilities, so the judges estimate the curve; the two [Schuur et al. 2022](https://in.nau.edu/news/country-of-permafrost/) estimates are marked on the card. An earlier assessment found [about 90 billion t by 2100 under high emissions, adding 0.13–0.27 °C, with 59% of all permafrost emissions coming after this century](https://www.carbonbrief.org/guest-post-what-the-latest-science-says-about-thawing-permafrost).

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Faster emission cuts limit thaw. Nothing can refreeze the Arctic at scale or recapture carbon that microbes have released.
