## Why it matters here

Water stress compares what people withdraw with the water a basin renews. Withdrawing 40 to 80% is high stress, over 80% extremely high ([Aqueduct 4.0](https://files.wri.org/d8/s3fs-public/2023-08/aqueduct-40-technical-note.pdf)). With no margin, even a [short drought](https://www.wri.org/insights/highest-water-stressed-countries) can empty taps and cut power and harvests. Being exposed is not the same as going without, which keeps this card at level 2 or below.

## In numbers

- **Today:** about [4 billion people, half of humanity](https://www.wri.org/insights/highest-water-stressed-countries), face high stress at least one month a year. 25 countries holding a quarter of humanity face extremely high stress every year.
- **2050:** the share could approach [60%](https://www.wri.org/research/aqueduct-40-updated-decision-relevant-global-water-risk-indicators), or 0.6 × [9.66 billion](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/population-with-un-projections) = 5.8 billion people. [1 billion more](https://www.wri.org/insights/highest-water-stressed-countries) would live with extremely high stress, and demand would rise 20 to 25%.
- **2080 and 2100:** Aqueduct projects every basin to [2080](https://files.wri.org/d8/s3fs-public/2023-08/aqueduct-40-technical-note.pdf) but publishes no world headcount for that year. Counting polluted water as unusable, one study finds [55% short of clean water today and up to 66% by 2100](https://communities.springernature.com/posts/the-global-clean-water-crisis-looms-large).

## What each level looks like

- **30 to 55%, level 1.** Our judgment: today's world. Shortages are seasonal or local, and fewer than 1 in 100 people lastingly lose water or food through stress alone.
- **55 to 70%, level 2.** Our judgment: WRI's 2050 world, with a billion more people in extreme stress, mostly where demand grows fastest. Rationing and lost harvests then reach over 1 in 100.
- **70 to 90%, level 2.** Our judgment. Today at most about 1 in 50 exposed people loses decent conditions (under 1% of humanity ÷ 50% exposed). Even if that rate tripled to 6%, 90% exposure gives 0.9 × 6% = 5.4%, below 1 in 10.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

WRI and the clean-water study publish scenarios, not chances, so the judges estimate the curve. The two measure different things: WRI counts quantity only. WRI also warns that water stress [cannot be measured directly](https://files.wri.org/d8/s3fs-public/2023-08/aqueduct-40-technical-note.pdf), so its results are not validated.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Cheap clean energy would make desalination and water recycling affordable for coastal cities. It cannot make rain fall inland, refill emptied aquifers quickly, or settle disputes over shared rivers.
