## Why it matters here

Batteries move solar power from midday into the night and replace engines in cars and trucks. Their price decides how much of the parent card, solar and wind with storage, can be delivered at any hour. It is a measure, not a potential level; its effect on people is counted in the parent card.

## In numbers

- BloombergNEF's (BNEF) yearly survey put the average pack at $1,436 per kWh in 2010 ([Canary Media](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/batteries/chart-lithium-ion-battery-prices-fall-yet-again)) and $108 in 2025 ([BNEF](https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-energy/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour)): 1,436 / 108 ≈ 13 times cheaper.
- The fall is not smooth. Prices rose for the first time in 2022, to $151 ([The Driven](https://thedriven.io/2023/11/28/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-hit-record-low-tipped-to-nearly-halve-again-by-2030/)). In 2025 packs cost $84 in China, while North American and European packs cost 44% and 56% more ([BNEF](https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-energy/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour)).
- The world made about 2,778 / 0.087 ≈ 31,900 TWh of electricity in 2025, solar's output over its share ([Ember](https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2026/2025-in-review/), [Ember](https://ember-energy.org/countries-and-regions/world/)), or 87 TWh a day. Storing a quarter of a day, 22 billion kWh, at $70 per kWh needs about $1.5 trillion for the packs alone: 15 years of the [$100 billion](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/energy/clean-energy-investment-saved-fuel-importers-260-billion-in-2025-iea) the IEA expects for battery storage in 2026.

## The lines on the card

- **$1,436, 2010**, the first year of BNEF's series.
- **$100, cars at parity**: the price often cited for electric cars costing the same as petrol cars ([Transport Topics](https://www.ttnews.com/articles/price-ev-battery-packs-falls)). Electric-car packs averaged $99 in 2025 (BNEF).
- **$70, grid-storage packs in 2025**, down 45% in a year (BNEF).

## Where the 2100 odds come from

No one forecasts pack prices to 2100. BNEF's own outlooks move: in 2023 it expected $80 by 2030 ([The Driven](https://thedriven.io/2023/11/28/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-hit-record-low-tipped-to-nearly-halve-again-by-2030/)), in 2024 $69 by 2030 ([Transport Topics](https://www.ttnews.com/articles/price-ev-battery-packs-falls)). For 2026 it expects only a 3% fall, to $105, because of raw-material costs and tariffs ([Gulf Times](https://www.gulf-times.com/article/716937/business/why-global-battery-prices-are-expected-to-drop-again-in-2026)).

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Suppose superintelligence arrives in the 2030s. Battery chemistry is a search through materials, which it could shorten: BNEF already names silicon and lithium-metal anodes, solid electrolytes and new cathodes as the next drivers. Each new chemistry still needs new factories, and new mines take almost [17 years](https://mining.ca/flippingbooks/mac-report-2023/53/) from discovery to production. Tariffs stay a political choice.
