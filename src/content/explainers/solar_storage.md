## Why it matters here

Energy is behind water, fertilizer, cooling and industry. In 2025 over 90% of new large renewable projects undercut the cheapest new fossil plant ([IRENA via PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/solar-pv-lcoe-ticks-marginally-upwards-2025-irena/)). Batteries and grids turn weather-driven sun and wind into power on demand. At a dominant share this card reaches level 4.

## In numbers

- In 2025 solar made 8.7% and wind 8.5% of the world's electricity, 17% together ([Ember](https://ember-energy.org/countries-and-regions/world/)). Solar's 2,778 TWh puts the total at 2,778 / 0.087 ≈ 31,900 TWh ([Ember](https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/global-electricity-review-2026/2025-in-review/)).
- Solar and wind grew by 841 TWh in 2025, 99% of demand growth (same source). Reaching 60% of today's use needs (0.60 − 0.17) × 31,900 ≈ 13,700 TWh more: 16 years at that pace, before demand grows.
- In Las Vegas, 5 kW of panels and a 17 kWh battery give 1 kW around the clock on an average day. Getting 97% of the way to constant supply costs $104 per MWh, against $118 for coal and $182 for new nuclear ([Ember](https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/solar-electricity-every-hour-of-every-day-is-here-and-it-changes-everything/)).

## What each level looks like

- **0 to 10%, level 1.** Our judgment: a side source while fossil fuels and hydro run the grids.
- **10 to 30%, level 2.** Our judgment, near today's 17%: off-grid solar benefits [560 million people](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/10/08/off-grid-solar-could-provide-first-time-electricity-access-to-almost-400-million-people-globally-by-2030), 560 / 8,060 million = 7% of [humanity](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL) in 2023, often as their first electricity.
- **30 to 60%, level 3.** Our judgment: the main source of power, cheap enough to lift over a tenth of humanity.
- **60 to 100%, level 4.** Our judgment: most power comes from the cheapest new source and fossil power is mostly gone, enough to lift over half of humanity a step. Level 5 would also need near-free storage.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

No source forecasts this share for 2100. The IEA's stated-policies scenario reaches [55% by 2050](https://www.powermag.com/the-age-of-electricity-and-5-other-forces-reshaping-the-global-energy-outlook/). Jean-Marc Jancovici [estimated in 2018](https://jancovici.com/en/energy-transition/renewables/100-renewable-electricity-at-no-extra-cost-a-piece-of-cake/) that wind with pumped-hydro storage would cost France about €4,000 billion over a century, against €600 billion for nuclear.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Suppose superintelligence arrives in the 2030s. Within a few years it could design better cells, batteries and grid software. Mines, factories and power lines still take years: the IEA puts a mine at almost [17 years](https://mining.ca/flippingbooks/mac-report-2023/53/) from discovery to production, and grid spending already reaches [$550 billion](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/energy/clean-energy-investment-saved-fuel-importers-260-billion-in-2025-iea) in 2026. The share rises at the pace of building.
