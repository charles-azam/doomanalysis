## Why it matters here

Oil is a third of world energy supply, 201 of 600 EJ in 2025 ([Energy Institute](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/au/pdf/2026/statistical-review-of-world-energy.pdf)), and Jancovici counts about 98% of world transport as running on it ([Jancovici](https://jancovici.com/en/energy-transition/societal-choices/a-couple-of-thoughts-on-the-energy-transition/)). This card counts harm from shortage only; burning oil is counted under climate.

## In numbers

- **Peak.** In 2010 the IEA put the peak of conventional crude in 2006, at 70 million barrels a day (mb/d) ([National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/101109-peak-oil-iea-world-energy-outlook)); in 2018 it put it in 2008, at 69 mb/d, with output 2.5 mb/d lower since ([Shift Project](https://theshiftproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/%C3%89tude_D%C3%A9clin-de-lapprovisionnement-de-lUE-en-p%C3%A9trole-dici-2030_TSP.pdf)).
- **The gap.** All oil liquids rose from 91.7 mb/d in 2015 to 100.6 in 2025; the US, mostly through shale, added 8.3 of the 8.9 ([Energy Institute](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/au/pdf/2026/statistical-review-of-world-energy.pdf)).
- **Harder oil.** Producing oil liquids takes 15.5% of their energy, and half by 2050 in one model ([Delannoy et al.](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/appene/v304y2021ics0306261921011673.html)): 1 ÷ 0.155 = 6.5 units out per unit put in, falling to 1 ÷ 0.5 = 2.
- **Reserves.** 1,802.5 billion barrels ÷ (100.4 mb/d × 365) = 49 years ([Eni](https://www.eni.com/content/dam/enicom/documents/eng/visione/wer/2026/02_WER-2026-Oil.pdf)). OPEC's crude-only figures give 1,570 ÷ (74.85 × 0.365) = 57 years ([Anadolu, citing OPEC](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/energy/oil/opecs-share-of-global-crude-output-ticks-higher-in-2025/56696)).
- **Importers.** India imports 86% of its oil, Europe 75%, China 73% ([Energy Institute](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/au/pdf/2026/statistical-review-of-world-energy.pdf)). The war from 28 February 2026 cut flows through the Strait of Hormuz from about 20 mb/d to a trickle; the record release of 400 million barrels of stocks covers 400 ÷ 20 = 20 days of them ([OGJ](https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/economics-markets/news/55363530/iea-middle-east-war-triggers-the-largest-supply-disruption-in-history)). By November the IEA counted 2.8 billion barrels lost ([Shafaq News](https://shafaq.com/en/Economy/IEA-Hormuz-disruptions-cut-oil-supply-by-2-8bn-barrels)).

## What each level looks like

- **0–25 mb/d, level 1.** Our judgment: by 2100 most uses can switch (over a quarter of new cars sold in 2025 were electric, [Energy Institute](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/au/pdf/2026/statistical-review-of-world-energy.pdf)); where they have not, the poorest lose fuel. Losing all oil would cut supply by a third, level 2 on the parent card.
- **25–70 mb/d, level 0.** Our judgment: oil kept for the hardest uses.
- **70–160 mb/d, level 0.** No scarcity.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

Nobody publishes odds for 2100. For 2050, OPEC forecasts demand of 124 mb/d ([OPEC](https://www.opec.org/pr-detail/1854607-18-june-2026.html)); the IEA has 114 under current policies and 96 under stated policies ([OGJ](https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/economics-markets/news/55420309/iea-sees-potential-for-looser-oil-lng-markets-after-hormuz-crisis)). Pathways limiting warming to 1.5 °C cut oil use by a median 60% from 2019 ([IPCC](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGIII_SPM.pdf)): 95.1 mb/d in 2019 ([Energy Institute](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/au/pdf/2026/statistical-review-of-world-energy.pdf)) × 0.4 = 38 mb/d. The Shift Project expects EU supply to fall 10–20% below 2019 over the 2030s ([Shift Project, 2021](https://theshiftproject.org/app/uploads/2025/02/The-Future-of-Oil-Supply_Shift-Project_May-2021_SUMMARY.pdf)).

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

It could speed the switch to electric transport. It cannot refill old fields or reopen a blockaded strait, and replacing vehicles and ships takes decades.
