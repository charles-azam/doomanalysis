## Why it matters here

Summer sea ice is the most visible gauge of warming. Its loss tracks global temperature, and so cumulative CO₂, almost in a straight line, and the IPCC finds [no tipping point for it](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_Chapter09.pdf). It can still feed other changes: the [Global Tipping Points Report 2025](https://wwfes.awsassets.panda.org/downloads/gtp_summary_report_2025-v4-pages-lo-res--1-.pdf) flags a possible vicious cycle in which ice retreat and permafrost thaw drive each other. The harm to people passes through warming and permafrost, which have their own cards, so this is a plain measure.

## In numbers

- **Today.** The 2026 minimum was [4.6 million km², tied for 10th-lowest on record](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/arctic-sea-ice-shrinks-to-its-2026-minimum/). Against the [1979–2000 average minimum of 6.70 million km²](https://nsidc.org/sea-ice-today/analyses/arctic-sea-ice-extent-settles-record-seasonal-minimum), that is (6.70 − 4.6) ÷ 6.70 ≈ 31% smaller.
- **Ice-free** means [under 1 million km²](https://www.colorado.edu/today/2024/03/05/arctic-could-become-ice-free-within-decade): 1 ÷ 6.70 ≈ 15% of the old September cover.
- **A carbon budget.** A practically ice-free September is likely [some years before another 1,000 billion tonnes of CO₂ are emitted after 2020](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_Chapter09.pdf).

## The lines on the card

- **1 million km², ice-free:** likely reached [at least once before 2050 in all five IPCC scenarios](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_SPM.pdf). With 2–3 °C of lasting warming it is [ice-free through September in most years](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_Chapter09.pdf).
- **3.41 million km²:** the [record low of 16 September 2012](https://nsidc.org/sea-ice-today/analyses/arctic-sea-ice-extent-settles-record-seasonal-minimum).
- **6.70 million km²:** the [1979–2000 average minimum](https://nsidc.org/sea-ice-today/analyses/arctic-sea-ice-extent-settles-record-seasonal-minimum).

## Where the 2100 odds come from

No source gives a probability for the 2100 value, so the judges estimate the chance of being above each line. Under high emissions, ice-free summers become [the usual state by late century](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_Chapter09.pdf). [Jahn and colleagues](https://www.colorado.edu/today/2024/03/05/arctic-could-become-ice-free-within-decade) expect a first ice-free day as soon as the 2020s or 2030s, ice-free conditions from August to October by late century under intermediate emissions, and up to nine ice-free months under the highest.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

The ice [comes back within about a decade if CO₂ is removed and the air cools](https://www.colorado.edu/today/2024/03/05/arctic-could-become-ice-free-within-decade), so superintelligence helps here only by lowering warming. Nothing refreezes the Arctic directly.
