## Why it matters here

Removing CO₂ helps only through the climate: it lowers future warming. Pathways that reach net zero still emit [5–16 Gt of CO₂ a year that removals elsewhere must balance](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGIII_SPM.pdf). Today [2.2 Gt a year is removed, 99.9% by forests and soils; newer methods remove 0.002 Gt](https://www.stateofcdr.org/asset/The-State-of-CDR-Executive-summary_Engish-June-2026.pdf). Each tonne cools only slightly, so level 3 needs removal on a huge scale.

## In numbers

- **Cooling.** Each 1,000 Gt of CO₂ means [about 0.45 °C of warming](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_SPM.pdf). If removal grows steadily from 2.2 Gt now to R in 2100, it removes 75 × (2.2 + R) ÷ 2 Gt by then.
- **People.** About [140 million more people face dangerous heat for every 0.1 °C](https://news.exeter.ac.uk/research/limiting-global-warming-to-1-5c-would-save-billions-from-dangerously-hot-climate), against [10.2 billion people in 2100](https://population.un.org/wpp/assets/Files/WPP2024_Summary-of-Results.pdf).
- **Energy.** Direct air capture, machines that filter CO₂ from the air, needs [at least 1.2 MWh of electricity per tonne: 10 Gt a year would take 12,000 TWh, over 40% of today's world electricity](https://news.mit.edu/2024/reality-check-tech-to-remove-carbon-dioxide-from-air-1120). At [$100–300 a tonne](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGIII_SPM.pdf), 10 Gt costs $1–3 trillion a year.

## What each level looks like

- **0.5–2 Gt, level 1.** Derived: at 2 Gt, 75 × 4.2 ÷ 2 = 157 Gt removed, × 0.45 ÷ 1,000 = 0.07 °C, sparing about 100 million people, 1%.
- **2–40 Gt, level 2.** Derived: at 40 Gt, 75 × 42.2 ÷ 2 = 1,580 Gt, or 0.7 °C, sparing about a billion, 10%.
- **40–100 Gt, level 3.** Derived: over a billion spared. All three are upper bounds: not everyone in dangerous heat loses decent living conditions.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

No source gives odds. The [State of CDR](https://www.stateofcdr.org/asset/State-of-Carbon-Dioxide-Removal-Edition-3_June2026.pdf) gives model medians for 2100: 12 Gt if countries meet their pledges, 15.3 Gt in the most ambitious 1.5 °C pathways, 23.6 Gt if action is delayed. For most single methods, its cautious estimates of what each could remove are [about 1 Gt a year](https://www.stateofcdr.org/asset/The-State-of-CDR-Executive-summary_Engish-June-2026.pdf).

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Suppose superintelligence arrives in the 2030s. Within years it could find better sorbents (materials that grab CO₂), cheaper ways to spread crushed rock that binds it, and reliable checks of what is stored. It cannot skip the build-out of plants, pipelines, wells and their power. At 15 Gt a year, direct air capture alone would need 15 billion tonnes × 1.2 MWh = 18,000 TWh or more.
