## Why it matters here

Electric cars took 39% of cobalt in 2025, and phones, laptops and jet-engine alloys most of the rest ([Cobalt Institute 2026](https://www.cobaltinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Cobalt-Market-Report-2025.pdf)). One country mines most of it, so the danger is being cut off. Cobalt-free batteries already hold a large share in China ([USGS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-cobalt.pdf)), so a shortfall raises costs more than it takes away essentials, and this card stops at level 1.

## In numbers

- **Years of reserves:** 12 Mt ÷ 0.31 Mt mined in 2025 = about 39 years. Known resources on land are about 25 Mt ([USGS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-cobalt.pdf)).
- **Risk of being cut off:** Congo, Indonesia and Russia mined 230 + 44 + 7.7 = 282 of 310 kt (91%), Congo alone 73% ([USGS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-cobalt.pdf)). China made 79% of refined cobalt ([Cobalt Institute 2026](https://www.cobaltinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Cobalt-Market-Report-2025.pdf)).
- **Demand:** 221 kt in 2024 and 330 kt in 2040 under today's policies. Planned mines cover 86% of 2035 needs, and the IEA sees a 50 kt deficit by 2040 as mines decline ([IEA 2025](https://sites.utexas.edu/mineralstransition/files/2025/05/Extract_GlobalCriticalMineralsOutlook2025-compressed.pdf)).
- **Recycling:** it met about 12% of demand in 2024, reaches 25% by 2040, and cuts the need for new mines by 35% by 2050 ([IEA 2025](https://sites.utexas.edu/mineralstransition/files/2025/05/Extract_GlobalCriticalMineralsOutlook2025-compressed.pdf)).

## What each level looks like

- **0 to 40%, level 0:** prices soar and batteries move to cobalt-free types. Our judgment, from the case on the card: Congo banned exports in February 2025, then capped them at 96,600 t a year for 2026 and 2027 ([USGS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-cobalt.pdf)). Hydroxide prices rose over 300% in 2025, and stocks outside Congo were drawn down ([Cobalt Institute 2026](https://www.cobaltinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Cobalt-Market-Report-2025.pdf)). In 2026, supply reaching the market is expected to fall 80 kt short, 27% of world demand ([Cobalt Institute update](https://www.cobaltinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Cobalt-Market-Update_Jan-May-2026.pdf)). Nobody has lost essentials.
- **40 to 100%, level 1:** planes, turbines and electronics stay scarce and dearer for years. Our judgment: substitutes cost performance or money ([USGS 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-cobalt.pdf)), but few of these uses are daily essentials.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

Nothing is published to 2100, so the judges estimate the curve. Most cobalt is a by-product of copper and nickel mines, so its supply follows theirs, and Congo's quotas show that policy now decides how much reaches the market ([Cobalt Institute 2026](https://www.cobaltinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Cobalt-Market-Report-2025.pdf)).

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Superintelligence could speed cobalt-free batteries and better recycling, which already cut demand. It cannot change Congo's export policy or make cobalt mines independent of copper and nickel.
