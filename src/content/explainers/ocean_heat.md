## Why it matters here

The ocean has absorbed [about 91% of the extra energy trapped by greenhouse gases; land took 5%, ice 3% and the air 1%](https://library.wmo.int/records/item/69807-state-of-the-global-climate-2025). That keeps the air cooler, but warmer water [damages sea life, feeds storms, speeds sea-ice loss and, with melting land ice, lifts the sea](https://library.wmo.int/records/item/69807-state-of-the-global-climate-2025). Those harms are counted on the warming, sea-level and coral cards, so this is a plain measure.

## In numbers

- **A zettajoule (ZJ) is 10²¹ joules.** Humanity used [600 EJ, or 0.6 ZJ, in 2025](https://www.connaissancedesenergies.org/statistical-review-of-world-energy-2026). The top 2,000 m of ocean gained [11.0–12.2 ZJ a year over 2005–2025, over twice the 1960–2005 rate](https://library.wmo.int/records/item/69807-state-of-the-global-climate-2025): 11.6 ÷ 0.6 ≈ 20 times humanity's energy use.
- **Today, about 480 ZJ since 1971.** The IPCC puts [1971–2018 at 396 ZJ](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_Chapter09.pdf). Adding about 11.6 ZJ a year in the top 2,000 m and [1 ZJ a year below](https://library.wmo.int/records/item/69807-state-of-the-global-climate-2025) for 2019–2025: 396 + 7 × 12.6 ≈ 484.
- **Gained by 1995–2014.** The climate system gained [282 ZJ over 1971–2006 and 152 ZJ over 2006–2018](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_SPM.pdf); 91% of 434 is 395, matching the IPCC's 396. The ocean's 91% of 282 is 257 ZJ by 2006. Less 1.5 years at 7 to 11.5 ZJ a year, the averages before and after 2006, leaves about 245 ZJ by mid-2004, the middle of 1995–2014.

## The lines on the card

From 1995–2014 to 2081–2100 the IPCC projects median gains of [1,180 ZJ under low emissions, 1,560 under the middle scenario and 2,230 under very high emissions](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_Chapter09.pdf). Adding the 245 ZJ already gained by then:

- **1,425 ZJ:** 245 + 1,180, low emissions (SSP1-2.6).
- **1,805 ZJ:** 245 + 1,560, the middle scenario (SSP2-4.5), close to current policies.
- **2,475 ZJ:** 245 + 2,230, very high emissions (SSP5-8.5).

These are averages over 2081–2100, so the 2100 values are somewhat higher.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

The IPCC gives [likely ranges, two to four times the 1971–2018 gain under low emissions and four to eight times under very high](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_Chapter09.pdf), but no probabilities, so the judges estimate the chance of passing each line. Deep-ocean warming [only starts to depend on the scenario after about 2040](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_Chapter09.pdf).

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

Faster emission cuts would slow the uptake later in the century. The heat already stored [stays for centuries to millennia, and the warming likely continues until at least 2300 even with low emissions](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_Chapter09.pdf).
