## Why it matters here

A large eruption kills people near the volcano. The wider harm comes from sulphur, which forms a haze high in the air that reflects sunlight. Summers turn cold and harvests fail far away: after Tambora in 1815, [food ran short in Europe and North America](https://eos.org/editors-vox/are-we-prepared-for-the-next-mega-eruption). Cassidy and Mani rate the odds of a volcanic catastrophe [hundreds of times](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/news-archive/2022/risk-of-volcano-catastrophe-a-roll-of-the-dice-say-experts) above those of an asteroid or comet strike, and nothing can stop an eruption.

## In numbers

- **Size.** Each step of the Volcanic Explosivity Index (VEI) is [ten times the last](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanic_explosivity_index): 10 km³ for VEI 6, 100 km³ for VEI 7, 1,000 km³ for VEI 8. Tambora threw out [about 100 km³](https://eos.org/editors-vox/are-we-prepared-for-the-next-mega-eruption).
- **10 km³ or more.** [Papale](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-25286-y) finds one VEI 6 per 72 years, one VEI 7 per 2,800 and one VEI 8 per 78,000: together one per 70 years, so 1 − e^(−75/70) = 66%.
- **Tambora-size or more.** Ice cores show [1.6 per thousand years](https://cp.copernicus.org/articles/18/485/2022/): 1 − e^(−0.0016 × 75) = 11%.
- **Super-eruption** (magnitude 8, a trillion tonnes erupted, close to VEI 8): [one per 17,000 years](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/publications/the-global-magnitude-frequency-relationship-for-large-explosive-v), 95% range 5,200 to 48,000. 75 ÷ 17,000 = 0.44%, range 0.16% to 1.4%.

## What each level looks like

- **1–300 km³, level 1.** Derived from Tambora: [about 100,000 deaths](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/news-archive/2022/risk-of-volcano-catastrophe-a-roll-of-the-dice-say-experts) among [1 billion people](https://eos.org/editors-vox/are-we-prepared-for-the-next-mega-eruption) is 0.01%. It cooled the world by [0.4–0.7 °C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1815_eruption_of_Mount_Tambora). The 300 km³ edge is our judgment.
- **300–1,000 km³, level 2.** Our judgment: several times Tambora's sulphur. In nuclear-winter models, where smoke dims the sun, 1.5 °C of peak cooling leaves [255 million people without food after two years](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43016-022-00573-0), within level 2.
- **Over 1,000 km³, level 3.** Our judgment. Toba, [at least 2,800 km³](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngest_Toba_eruption), is modelled to have cooled the north by [4 °C or more](https://www.cam.ac.uk/research/news/early-humans-were-sheltered-from-worst-effects-of-volcanic-supereruption). The next nuclear case leaves [926 million without food](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43016-022-00573-0). Toba's cooling [fades within about five years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youngest_Toba_eruption), not ten, and early humans were [sheltered from the worst](https://www.cam.ac.uk/research/news/early-humans-were-sheltered-from-worst-effects-of-volcanic-supereruption), so we stop short of level 4.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

All three points are yearly rates turned into 75-year chances. Sources disagree. Papale's geological counts give only 2.7% for Tambora-size and 0.1% for a super-eruption, while ice cores find large eruptions [twice as often as once thought](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/news-archive/2022/risk-of-volcano-catastrophe-a-roll-of-the-dice-say-experts). Cassidy and Mani's [1 in 6 per century](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/news-archive/2022/risk-of-volcano-catastrophe-a-roll-of-the-dice-say-experts) for magnitude 7 gives 1 − (5/6)^0.75 = 13%. We adopt the ice-core rate: it counts eruptions by sulphur, which is what cools the climate.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

It could forecast eruptions better and design food that grows without sunlight. It cannot stop an eruption, and feeding billions through dark years needs stocks and factories built beforehand.
