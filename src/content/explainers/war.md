## Why it matters here

War kills directly and wrecks the farms, power, water and hospitals that keep others alive. A conventional war stays at level 1 or 2. A nuclear war can reach level 4: smoke from burning cities dims the sun for years and harvests fail, a "nuclear winter".

## In numbers

- The Second World War killed [60 to 75 million](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II_casualties), 3% of the 2.3 billion then alive. Among today's [8.2 billion](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL), that toll is 0.8%.
- In [Xia et al. 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43016-022-00573-0), if trade stops, 100 small warheads on cities kill 27 million and starve 255 million: 282 million of 6.7 billion, 4%. With 4,400 larger ones, 360 million die and 5.3 billion starve: 85%.

## What each level looks like

The level is the share killed. Hunger reaches more people than it kills, but mostly for a few years, so it does not raise the levels.

- **0.01 to 1%, level 1.** Up to 80 million dead, eight times the [10.4 million](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/deaths-in-state-based-conflicts-by-type) killed in all battles from 1946 to 2025.
- **1 to 10%, level 2.** A world war, or 100 warheads on cities (4%).
- **10 to 50%, level 3.** 250 to 500 warheads: 15 to 40% killed or starved (Xia et al.).
- **50 to 99%, level 4.** A full United States–Russia war (85%).
- **Over 99%, level 5.** Our judgment, as no study models it: under 80 million survivors are unlikely to rebuild within a century.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

[Clauset 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5834001/) gives 43% per century for a war with the Second World War's 16.6 million battle deaths, if wars stay as likely as in 1823–2003. Its total toll was about four times that: 0.8% of humanity today. Over 74 years, 1 − 0.57^0.74 = 34%.

In the [XPT tournament](https://forecastingresearch.org/pdf/existential-risk-persuasion-tournament.pdf) of 2022, superforecasters gave 4% for nuclear weapons killing over 10% of humanity by 2100, nuclear experts 8%; for extinction, 0.074% and 0.55%. Nuclear only, so floors.

A [2024 survey](https://opennuclear.org/open-nuclear-network/publication/can-humanity-achieve-century-nuclear-peace) gave 5% (experts) and 1% (superforecasters) for nuclear weapons killing 10 million by 2045. [Stephen Clare](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/great-power-conflict/) puts a great-power war by 2100 at 45%.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

It could improve early warning, arms-control checks and famine relief, or give one side an edge that tempts it to strike first. It does not remove the motives for war, the warheads already built, or the physics of smoke and harvests.
