## Why it matters here

Nature must stumble on a germ that both spreads easily and kills; people can pick, alter or leak one. This card counts pandemics started by a laboratory or vaccine accident, or released by a group or one person. None has passed level 1, yet forecasters see it as [the main road](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/9ddheDChiMhveCfSw/what-do-xpt-results-tell-us-about-biorisk) to extinction by disease.

## In numbers

- **Worst so far:** the 1977 flu, whose virus nearly matched 1950s strains, so it probably came from a vaccine trial or a laboratory (unproven). It killed under 5 per 100,000 people, under 0.005% ([Rozo and Gronvall 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4542197/)).
- **Yearly risk:** in 2025, 46 experts gave [0.3%](https://forecastingresearch.org/ai-enabled-biorisk) for a human-caused epidemic starting in 2028 that kills over 100,000 people or costs $1 trillion. Held to 2100: 1 − 0.997^74 = 20%. Among [8.3 billion people](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/population-with-un-projections), 100,000 is 0.0012%.
- **Laboratories:** one study gives [0.016% to 0.8% a year](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5576214/) for a pandemic from risky virus research, by accident or misuse: 1 − 0.99984^74 = 1% to 1 − 0.992^74 = 45% by 2100, a width that shows how little is known.

## What each level looks like

- **Under 1%, level 1 (derived).** A leak or an attack kills up to 100 million. COVID-19 [killed 0.3%](https://ourworldindata.org/historical-pandemics); [WHO's advisers](https://www.who.int/news/item/27-06-2025-who-scientific-advisory-group-issues-report-on-origins-of-covid-19) lean to a jump from animals but could not settle its origin.
- **1 to 10%, level 2 (derived).** The 1918 flu's scale, never yet caused by people.
- **10 to 50%, level 3 (derived).** One person in ten to one in two dies.
- **50 to 99%, level 4.** Our judgment: most die, but enough survive to rebuild.
- **Above 99%, level 5.** Our judgment: too few survivors to rebuild within a century.

## Where the 2100 odds come from

From the [XPT tournament](https://forecastingresearch.org/pdf/existential-risk-persuasion-tournament.pdf) of 2022: its superforecasters gave 4% for a genetically engineered pathogen killing over 1% of humanity by 2100, 0.8% for over 10% and 0.01% for extinction. Its biorisk experts gave 10.25%, 3% and 1%. We adopt the larger group. The match is approximate: the XPT also counts releases by states, and an unaltered germ escaping a laboratory counts as natural. Toby Ord is gloomier, at [1 in 30](https://forecastingresearch.org/pdf/existential-risk-persuasion-tournament.pdf) for existential catastrophe from engineered pandemics by 2120.

## What superintelligence can and cannot change

The 2025 experts raised their yearly risk to [1.5%](https://forecastingresearch.org/ai-enabled-biorisk), or 67% if held to 2100, if AI matched a top team of virologists on a troubleshooting test, which it since has. In another scenario, AI safeguards and compulsory screening of DNA orders cut 1.25% to 0.4%. Stronger AI helps attackers and defenders alike.
